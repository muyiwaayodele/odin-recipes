Sausage and Potato Bake

Description

This sausage and potato bake is an italian comfort food classic

if you're looking for a comforting meal that is made with ingredients you currently have in the fridge, then you've found the best recipes for you

Ingredients

The ingredients in this bake are as follows

  • Olive oil
  • Italian Sausage
  • Potatoes
  • Bell Peppers
  • Onion
  • White Wine
  • Chicken Stock
  • Seasonings

Steps

This are the steps needed for making the dish

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F(200 degrees C).
  3. Heat 2 teaspoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, and cook and stir the sausage until browned, 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer cooked sausage to a large baking dish.
  4. Pour 1/4 cup of olive oil into the skillet, and cook potatoes, stirring occasionally until browned, about 10 minutes.
  5. Place the potato into the baking dish, leaving some oil in the skillet.
  6. Cook and stir green and red peppers and onion in the hot skillet until they are beginning to soften, about 5 minutes.
  7. Add the vegetables to the baking dish. Pour wine and chicken stock over the vegetables and sausage and sprinkle with italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
  8. Gently stir sausage, potatoes and vegetables together.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven until hot and bubbling, 20 minutes. Serve hot.
