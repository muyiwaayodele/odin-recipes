Fricase de Pollo

Description

One of the favorite meal from cuba

Serve with frijoles negros and arros blanco(black rice and beans)

Ingredients

The ingredients in this meal is as follows;

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • half large red bell pepper, diced
  • half red onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups water, or as needed
  • 6 skinless chicken leg quarters, seperated into thighs and drumsticks
  • salt to taste

Steps

These are the steps needed for preparing the dish;

  1. Heat olive oil in a pressure cooker over medium heat. Stir in red bell pepper and onion; cook and stir until the onion is softened and turned translucent, 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook until golden
  2. Dissolve tomato paste in 1 cup water and pour it into the pressure cooker. Place chicken thighs and drumsticksin the pan so the pieces are evenly spaced. Add enough water so that the chicken is nearly covered.
  3. Seal the pressure cooker and bring up to pressure over high heat. Reduce the heat to low, maintaing full pressure; cook for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and allow the pressure to drop naturally, about 20 minutes Season chicken with salt to taste.
